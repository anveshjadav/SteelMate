## Full Description (≤4000 chars)

**SteelCalc — Structural Steel Weight Calculator (IS 808 : 1989)**

Calculate the weight of structural steel members in seconds, straight from the official IS 808 : 1989 unit-mass tables. Built for structural engineers, fabricators, estimators, quantity surveyors, and students — no more thumbing through handbooks or cross-checking PDF tables.

**How it works (3 simple steps)**
1. **Select a member** — choose the section type and designation
2. **Set inputs** — length, number of members, and material of construction
3. **Get the result** — total weight in kg, tonnes, and grams, with a full calculation breakdown

**Everything uses the standard formula: W = M × L × N**
(Unit mass × Length × Number of members), with the actual values shown so you can verify every figure by hand.

**Section types covered (IS 808 : 1989)**
- Beams: ISMB, ISJB, ISLB, ISWB
- Columns: ISSC, ISHB
- Channels: ISMC, ISMCP, ISJC, ISLC
- Angles: ISA equal leg, ISA unequal leg
- Flat plates (IS 1730) — weight computed from width, thickness, and material density

**Materials of construction (MOC)**
- IS 2062 Gr. A / B / C (structural steel)
- IS 8500 (high-strength low-alloy)
- SS 304 & SS 316 (stainless)
- IS 1030 (cast carbon steel), IS 2002 (boiler quality)
- Al 6061 (aluminium), Copper
Each with its correct density and applicable standard.

**Key features**
- Full section-property display: depth, flange width, web/flange thickness, and unit mass (kg/m) for every selection
- Instant results in kg, tonnes, and grams
- Quick-count buttons (1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100) for fast entry
- Share/export any calculation as a clean, formatted summary
- Live unit-mass preview while typing plate dimensions
- Clean dark & light themes
- Simple 3-step wizard with progress indicator — no clutter, no learning curve

**Why SteelCalc**
- Accurate to the official Indian Standard tables — no internet needed
- Saves time on site, in the office, or in the classroom
- Free, lightweight, and works offline

*Note: SteelCalc is provided for educational, learning, and reference purposes only. Results should not be used as the sole basis for engineering design, fabrication, construction, or safety-critical decisions — always verify against OEM data and your project specifications.*
